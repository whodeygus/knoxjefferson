About

Built on principle.

Knox Jefferson Group LLC is a Tennessee-based management consulting and project delivery firm. We specialize in organizing, orchestrating, and executing complex initiatives for federal agencies and private organizations — managing the full scope of work from planning through completion.

Whether the engagement is a federal contract requiring disciplined project oversight or a private initiative demanding coordinated subcontractor management, Knox Jefferson Group brings the same standard of leadership and accountability to every assignment.

"Knox and Jefferson are more than county names to us — they represent the tradition of American leadership, accountability, and service that we carry into every engagement."
